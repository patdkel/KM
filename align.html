How you'll line it up

  1. Roll the coffee cart out of the corner by the stairs door.
  2. Mark 1: the floor in the SW corner, where the stairs-door wall (Door B) meets the entry-door wall (Door A). Aim the crosshair right where the two baseboards meet.
  3. Mark 2: the floor at the foot of one of those walls, as far from the corner as you can. Choose which wall:

Tip: move the phone slowly around the room for a few seconds before marking, so it maps the floor well. Back to the quick viewer.
